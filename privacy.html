Blood-Connect Privacy Notice

Effective Date: 2026

Blood-Connect respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information that you provide while using the Blood-Connect mobile application.

Important: Blood-Connect collects and processes information only for purposes related to providing, maintaining, securing, and improving the application's services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, Blood-Connect may collect information such as:

  • Name and personal details
  • Email address
  • Contact information
  • Address or location information provided by the user
  • Blood type
  • Information related to blood requests
  • Information related to donor applications
  • Government-issued identification information submitted for verification
  • Images submitted during verification
  • Account and authentication information

2. How We Use Your Information

Information collected through Blood-Connect may be used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Process blood requests.
  • Process donor applications.
  • Perform identity verification.
  • Facilitate potential blood donor matching.
  • Communicate important application notifications.
  • Maintain application security.
  • Prevent fraudulent or abusive activity.
  • Improve the application's functionality and reliability.

3. Identity Verification Information

Blood-Connect may require users to submit identification documents for identity verification.

Identification information and submitted images may be reviewed by authorized administrators for the purpose of verifying the identity of the account holder.

Identity verification is used to confirm account information and does not constitute medical clearance or approval to donate blood.

4. Blood Request Information

When creating a blood request, users may provide information such as blood type, number of units required, urgency, hospital information, contact information, and other request details.

Some information may be made available to appropriate users or administrators when necessary to facilitate blood donation coordination.

5. Donor Information

Users applying to become donors may provide information such as blood type, donation history, availability, and other information required for donor application processing.

Donor information may be reviewed by authorized administrators during the application review process.

6. How Information May Be Shared

Blood-Connect may make relevant information available to authorized administrators and appropriate users when necessary to provide the application's blood coordination services.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal processes, or legitimate requests from authorized authorities.

7. Third-Party Services

Blood-Connect may use third-party services to provide certain application functionality, such as authentication, database services, image processing, hosting, notifications, and other technical services.

These third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

8. Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures may be used to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Data Retention

Personal information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application's services, maintain records, perform verification, resolve disputes, comply with applicable requirements, and maintain security.

10. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable laws and regulations, users may have rights relating to their personal information, including rights to request access to, correction of, or other appropriate handling of their personal information.

Privacy-related requests may be submitted through the official Blood-Connect contact channel.

11. Children's Privacy

Blood-Connect is not intended to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws or regulations.

Users should provide information only when they are legally permitted to use the application and provide such information.

12. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The Blood-Connect mobile application may use technologies necessary for authentication, security, application functionality, and service operation.

13. Changes to This Privacy Notice

This Privacy Notice may be updated when changes are made to the application, services, data processing practices, or applicable requirements.

Updated versions may be published through the Blood-Connect application or official website.

14. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Notice or the handling of your personal information, please contact the Blood-Connect project administrators through the official contact information provided by the project.

15. Applicable Law

This Privacy Notice is intended to operate in accordance with applicable laws and regulations of the Republic of the Philippines.
